Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

1. Overview

JawScore is an on-device face analysis app. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. All face scanning, scoring, and history storage happen locally on your device. This policy explains exactly what face data the app processes, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

2. Face Data — What the App Collects

When you perform a 3D Scan (ARKit TrueDepth camera) or a Photo Scan (a portrait you choose from your photo library), the app processes camera frames or the selected photo in memory only to derive:

  • Facial geometry measurements — numerical values such as distances, ratios, and symmetry measures (48 measurements in total), computed by Apple's Vision and ARKit frameworks on your device;
  • A capture-quality value — a number describing lighting and framing of the frame;
  • A derived score — the overall score, confidence band, and eight dimension scores computed by the app's on-device scoring engine.

The app does not collect: raw camera frames or depth maps after scoring completes; face images in your scan history; biometric templates (no Face ID data is ever accessed — Face ID is handled solely by the operating system); or any identifiers linked to your face.

2.1 TrueDepth API — What It Provides and How It Is Used

On devices with a TrueDepth camera, JawScore uses Apple's ARKit ARFaceTrackingConfiguration (the TrueDepth API). While the scan screen is open, ARKit provides, frame by frame:

  • a 3D face mesh (about 1,220 vertices) and its triangle indices, describing the shape of your face;
  • the camera image of the current frame, which JawScore reads as a pixel buffer only to estimate brightness, lighting variance, and texture uniformity (used to judge capture quality);
  • the face anchor transform (position and orientation) and the camera transform, used only to estimate distance from the camera and head tilt;
  • an ambient light intensity estimate.

All of these are consumed in real time and in memory. The app keeps, at most, the single best frame of the current scan while the scan screen is open, in order to compute the measurements below. When the scan finishes or the scan screen is closed, the mesh, the camera frames, and the pixel buffers are released from memory. No mesh, depth map, or camera frame is written to disk, to iCloud, or to any server. The TrueDepth API is not used for facial recognition, identity verification, authentication, or advertising, and the app does not create or store a face template or "faceprint".

From the best frame, the app computes 48 numerical geometry measurements (for example ratios, angles, and left/right symmetry values). These numbers, the capture-quality value, and the resulting scores are the only face-related data that persists.

3. Face Data — Use

The face data described in Sections 2 and 2.1 is used for one purpose only: to produce your jawline/facial scores and personal progress history inside the app. It is never used for advertising, profiling, identification, authentication, or any machine-learning training.

4. Face Data — Storage and Retention

Only the numerical measurements and scores (no images, no raw frames, no mesh, no depth data) are stored, in the app's local database (SwiftData) inside the app's sandbox on your device. Where it is stored: on your device only — never on a JawScore server and never in iCloud, because JawScore has no server and no cloud sync.

How long it is retained: there is no fixed retention period. A scan record is kept until you delete it (swipe to delete in Trends), until you use Take a Break, or until you uninstall the app, at which point all data is permanently removed from your device. Nothing is stored in the cloud.

Camera frames, depth maps, face meshes, and the in-memory preview image exist only transiently during a scan/session and are discarded from memory when the session ends or the app terminates.

5. Face Data — Sharing With Third Parties

By default, face data never leaves your device. There are exactly two user-initiated exceptions:

  • Optional Deep Analysis with your own DeepSeek API key. If — and only if — you enter your personal DeepSeek API key in Settings and then tap "Deep Analysis" on a result and confirm the per-request dialog ("Send a photo to DeepSeek? Your face data will leave your device this one time."), the app sends the single captured preview photo to DeepSeek's API (api.deepseek.com) to generate coaching text. The photo is sent over HTTPS directly from your device to DeepSeek using your own key; JawScore has no server and never sees it. DeepSeek's handling of that request is governed by DeepSeek's own privacy policy. The app does not retain the photo after the request, and no analysis happens without your explicit confirmation each time.
  • Saving your result card to your photo library. If you tap the save button on the result card, the rendered card image is saved to your own photo library at your request (using the standard iOS "Add to Photos" permission). Nothing is uploaded.

Apart from the two opt-in actions above, JawScore does not sell, rent, or share face data or any other data with any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The optional "Contact Support" form sends only the text you type (subject, name, email, message) to the support endpoint — never any photo or face data.

Apple system frameworks: ARKit and Vision run entirely on your device as part of iOS. The developer of JawScore does not operate a server, does not receive your face data, and does not send it to Apple. Using these frameworks does not transmit face data to the developer or to third parties.

6. Face Data — Deletion

You can delete individual scan records from the Trends tab at any time, which removes the associated measurements from the local database. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data, including all measurements and scores, permanently. For the optional DeepSeek request, retention of the transmitted photo is governed by DeepSeek's privacy policy (https://www.deepseek.com/en/privacy); JawScore itself retains no copy.

7. Permissions

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription) — used only for the live 3D scan. On TrueDepth devices this includes the ARKit face-tracking (TrueDepth) session described in Section 2.1. Frames are used for the scan and then discarded.
  • Add to Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription) — used only when you explicitly save a result card.
  • Notifications — optional weekly rescan reminder, delivered locally on-device.

8. Other Data

All other data (habits, plan entries, settings, your BYO API key) is stored locally on your device. Your DeepSeek API key, if you provide one, is stored in the device Keychain and is sent only to api.deepseek.com when you trigger a Deep Analysis. If you use the Contact Support form, the text fields you submit are delivered to the support team to answer your request.

9. Children

JawScore is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new policy at this page.

11. Face Data — Direct Answers

Q: What face data does JawScore collect?
A: Only numerical results: 48 geometry measurements (distances, ratios, angles, symmetry), a capture-quality value, and the derived scores. On TrueDepth devices, ARKit face-mesh vertices, camera frames, and depth information are processed in memory and discarded after the scan; they are never stored.

Q: Will face data be shared with third parties?
A: No, by default and with one opt-in exception. Face data stays on the device. The only third-party transfer is the optional "Deep Analysis" feature, which sends one preview photo to api.deepseek.com — only after the user enters their own DeepSeek API key and confirms a per-request dialog each time. JawScore has no server and never receives the photo.

Q: Where is face data stored?
A: In the app's local database (SwiftData) inside the app sandbox on the user's device. No cloud storage, no developer-side storage.

Q: How long is face data retained?
A: No fixed period. Measurements and scores are kept until the user deletes the scan record, uses Take a Break, or uninstalls the app. In-memory meshes, frames, and depth data are discarded at the end of each scan session.

Q: How can the user delete face data?
A: Swipe to delete a scan in the Trends tab, use the Take a Break option, or uninstall the app (Section 6).
